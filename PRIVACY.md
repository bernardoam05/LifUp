# Privacy policy · Política de privacidade · Política de privacidad · Politique de confidentialité · Datenschutzerklärung

_Last updated: November 2026_

## English

CoreQuest does not sell any personal data and has no account. Everything you do in the app (missions, progress, settings, statistics) is stored only on your phone, with one optional exception: pair missions, described below.

- **Permissions** are used on the device only:
  - *Usage access* and *display over other apps* block the apps you choose during focus sessions and measure screen time for missions.
  - *Location* tracks the distance of runs and walks you start.
  - *Physical activity* counts steps.
  - *Notifications*, *exact alarms*, *ignore battery optimisations* and *autostart* deliver reminders and close the day at midnight.
  - None of these readings leave the phone.
- **Backups** are files you export yourself (or the app's automatic daily copies in its own folder). Where you keep them is up to you.
- **Purchases**, if any, are handled by Google Play. The app only learns whether a purchase was made, never your payment details.
- **Crash reports** are never sent automatically. If the app crashes it offers to share the report, which you can read first and send wherever you choose.
- **Pair missions** (only if you create or join one): to share progress with your partner, the app signs in anonymously to Google Cloud Firestore (Google, servers in the EU) and stores there a random ID, your hero's name and level, the mission, its schedule and time zone, and when you completed each of your parts. Only the two people in the pair can read it, it travels encrypted, and it is deleted when you leave the pair. The "I'm done" link you can send carries the same completion; what's in it stays on the phones and is not sent to any server.
- **Internet** is only used for pair missions (above) and to read the release notes of new versions from GitHub (and, in the version installed from GitHub, to download its updates). Nothing about you is sent.
- **Children:** the app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting the app deletes all its data. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bernardoam05/LifUp/issues.
