Turn your habits into an RPG. Every day brings a set of missions — exercise, sleep, focus, study, home — and keeping them earns XP, gold and attributes. Missing them has a real cost. No account, no ads: everything stays on your phone.

<b>Daily missions that grow with you</b>
• Missions drawn from a catalogue of 95, with a load that rises as you keep your days
• Optional missions (meditation, swimming, an instrument, medication…) wait in the Forge until you turn them on
• Easy, Normal or Hard: you choose how much a missed day hurts
• Rest days each week, and pauses for holidays or illness that don't break your streak

<b>Focus that actually blocks</b>
• Timed focus sessions that block the apps you pick
• Do Not Disturb and ambient sound (rain, fire or your own)
• A quick-settings tile to start in one tap

<b>A real game</b>
• A weekly boss, weak to one family of missions — 27 of them
• Portals that open during the day with double rewards
• 117 echoes to collect, evolve and equip, each with its own passive; the shop has 3 new ones every day
• Hunter ranks from E to SS and 27 achievements

<b>Meet Core</b>
Your companion grows with your rank through five forms, from Potential to Legend, and her face follows your day: focused, relaxed, asleep at night, happy when you get it all done.

<b>Less tapping</b>
• Steps and GPS tick some missions by themselves
• Widgets: today's missions, focus and progress
• Daily, weekly and monthly reports, plus an activity calendar

<b>Yours only</b>
• No account, no server, no ads, no tracking
• Back up to a file whenever you like, plus automatic daily copies
• In English, Portuguese, Spanish, French and German
